A little about me

A little about the woman behind the words.

I'm Elena, a novelist drawn to the quiet moments that often say the most. The conversations left unfinished, the memories we carry without noticing, and the small decisions that change the shape of a life are where many of my stories begin.

My work explores the ties that bind us, the truths we learn to live with, and the courage it takes to begin again.

"I am interested in the things people don't always say, but never quite forget."

When the Rain Remembers

Literary Fiction 2025 Hardcover · Paperback · eBook

Some families keep their secrets in photographs. Others bury them in silence. When Amara returns to the home she thought she had outgrown, a collection of old letters begins to unravel the history her family never knew how to tell.

A tender story about mothers and daughters, memory and forgiveness, and the complicated meaning of home.
